👥 Who Does What
Person A
BST Toolkit (Algorithmic Core)
node.py bst.py registry.py rebuild.py

Builds the entire BST engine. Every other module depends on this. Requires understanding of recursion, BST insert/delete/search, and divide & conquer (balanced rebuild).

Person B
Data + ML Toolkit
data/download.py grid_search.py transfer.py

Downloads both datasets, runs the hyperparameter grid search using itertools.product, and performs the transfer analysis comparing rankings between Dataset A and B.

Person C
Benchmarks + Notebook
timer.py capstone.ipynb

Builds the timing utilities, handles integration testing, and writes all 8 sections of the final Jupyter notebook deliverable. Also runs the benchmark comparison of 3 rebuild strategies.
